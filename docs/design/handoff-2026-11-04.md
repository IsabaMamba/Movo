## Backend work, in build order

Every new table and function: `revoke all … from public, anon`, RPC-only writes.

1. **Notifications (Avisos v3).** New rows in `notifications`:
   - `chat_reply` and `chat_organizer` — can be turned off per chat;
   - `chat_message_removed` — cites the rule, never who removed it;
   - `blocked_person_going` — never names the person;
   - `activity_cancelled_weather` — CNE alert, water sports only;
   - `terms_updated`.
   The lock-screen push stays the fixed sentence «Tienes un aviso nuevo en Movo».
2. **Rules (Normas v4).** Rule 29 «El chat, igual que en persona» and rule 30 «Tres reglas fijas en
   el agua» in a new section «En el chat y en el agua», so nothing is renumbered. Rule 11's text
   changes. `RULES_VERSION = '2026-11-04'`; «Qué cambió» lists 15 changes.
3. **Chat entry points.** A chat icon with the unread count in Mis sesiones' top bar, a «Chat de la
   sesión» row in session detail, and a «Chat» tab in the group page. No entry from a profile:
   there are no private messages.
4. **Group chat moderation (Chat v2).**
   - Remove: soft delete with `removed_by` and `removed_reason` (a rule number or the group's rules),
     visible to staff for 30 days. The author gets `chat_message_removed`.
   - Pin: `pinned_at` on `messages`, at most 3 per group.
   - Who can write: a column on `communities` (everyone | organizers only), checked in the send RPC.
5. **Group chat instead of a link (Grupos v5/v6).** The planned outside-chat-link field on groups is
   not needed.
6. **Perfil v3.**
   - Email: `supabase.auth.updateUser({ email })` with «Secure email change» on (confirms on the new
     address and notifies the old one).
   - Password: reauthenticate, `updateUser({ password })`, 8 characters minimum, then sign out other
     sessions (`scope: 'others'`), on by default.
   - Bloqueos: the list from `blocks` with the public name only, never `profile_private`.
   - Signed in with Google or Apple: no password row; the email is changed with the provider.
7. **Web.** `+not-found.tsx` for the 404 and an error boundary per route for «Algo falló», with a
   short error code that is logged without personal data. Both `noindex`. A session link that no
   longer exists falls into the 404 and never says why. `/ayuda` and `/terminos` are public and
   indexable.
8. **Accessibility (Accesibilidad v1).**
   - Control borders (inputs, unselected chips, off toggles, radios, ghost buttons) move from
     `#3B445B` to `#66718C` (3.2–3.9:1). Dividers stay `#2A344A`.
   - New `familyText.Montaña = #F2705F` when «Senderismo» is written in its color; dots and icons
     keep `#E44C3D`.
   - 12 px minimum (initials, the «consola» badge, map credit). Tab bar labels stay at 11.
   - No fixed `height` on rows with text — `minHeight`. `allowFontScaling` stays on everywhere;
     `maxFontSizeMultiplier = 1` only on tab bar labels (with the iOS large content viewer). Test on
     an iPhone SE at 200 % and on Android with the largest font and display size.
9. **Emails (Correos v1).** Paste the templates into Supabase › Auth › Email Templates using
   `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}` and `{{ .Token }}` (confirm signup, reset password, change email to both
   addresses). «Password changed» and «account deleted» are sent from an Edge Function if Supabase
   doesn't provide them. Before launch: custom SMTP (the default sender is for testing only) with
   SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the domain. Inline styles and tables; test Gmail and Outlook in dark mode.
