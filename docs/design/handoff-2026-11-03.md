## Decisions taken today

- **19 sports, six families.** Correr (Running, Trail running), Montaña (Senderismo), Balón (Fútbol,
  Voleibol, Baloncesto, Ultimate frisbee, Touch rugby, Sóftbol, Béisbol), Ruedas (Ciclismo, Ciclismo
  de montaña), Entrenar (Yoga, Bootcamp, Calistenia, Zumba y baile), Agua (Surf, Kayak, Rafting).
  One color per family; the sport's name always goes next to it. Lilac stays reserved for
  women-only.
- **Water sports** carry three fixed rules the organizer can't remove: a life jacket for everyone,
  everyone can swim, and the session is cancelled if the CNE declares an alert for the area.
- **Location and notifications are asked at sign-up.** Location is approximate, only while Movo is
  in use, and only the district is stored. Our explainer screen leads straight into the system
  dialog with one button (App Store guideline 5.1.1(iv)); declining works, and the person types
  their area instead.
- **Passwords need 8 characters or more.**
- **Chat lives inside Movo** so nobody shares a phone number. Session chats and group chats only;
  no private messages.
- **No special suspension flow.** «Sin fecha» (no end date) is final; reviews, if any, are case by
  case once a contact channel exists.
- **Help without contact** for now; contact page and form later.
- **«Modo solo» is obsolete.**
- **No light theme.**

## Backend work, in build order

Every new table and function: `revoke all … from public, anon`, RPC-only writes.

1. **Sports catalog.**
   - `categories` gains `family`; 16 new rows, each with its `attribute_schema` (table in Deportes v1).
   - Profiles store (category, level) per sport.
   - `communities` allow up to 3 categories; a session still has one.
   - Water categories get the three fixed rules as required, non-editable fields.
2. **Sign-up v2.**
   - Apple and Google sign-in.
   - «Sobre ti» shows when the profile has no birthdate; the minimum password is 8 (Supabase and screen).
   - Location: iOS `requestWhenInUseAuthorization`; Android `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`. Resolve
     the district with `locations_set_zone`, store it in `profile_private`, and never store
     coordinates.
   - Notifications: Android `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`.
   - Record `location` and `push` in `consents` with a date.
3. **Chat.**
   - `messages` exists (0001, 0003, 0020). Add Realtime.
   - Hide messages from blocked people (`is_blocked`).
   - A client-side nudge before sending a phone number (nothing stored).
   - Session chats close 24 h after the session ends (read-only) and are deleted after 30 days; group
     messages older than 6 months are deleted.
   - Organizers can soft-delete messages in their group.
   - A notification preference per chat; the default is replies and organizer messages.
   - Entry points:
     - a chat button in session detail;
     - a chat icon in Mis sesiones;
     - a «Chat» tab in the group page.
4. **Blocking.**
   - `block_user` / `unblock_user`.
   - Filter with `is_blocked` in Descubrir, profiles, invitations, chat and lists.
   - When someone blocked already goes to a session, show the notice in detail without naming them.
   - A Bloqueos list in Perfil.
5. **Reports.**
   - New reasons `suplantacion` and `not_eligible` (the latter only in women-only spaces).
   - The session where it happened is stored as context; «Bloquear también» calls `block_user`.
6. **Staff.**
   - `suspend_account` accepts a null duration = no end date.
   - The queue orders by reason priority (`not_eligible` and `comportamiento` first), then by age.
   - Places: «Devolver para corregir» with a note.
7. **«Llegué» without signal.**
   - Store the activity, code and tap time on the phone.
   - On reconnect, `self_check_in` takes that time and accepts it inside the window and before closing.
   - Mark it `checked_in_by = self_offline`; the organizer confirms it.
8. **Pasadas.** A nightly job that keeps session detail for 6 months, then a monthly count.
9. **Help.** FAQs in a file per language; public `/ayuda`. «¿Te sirvió?» stores question, yes/no and
   reason only.
10. **Legal pages.**
    - Routes `/terminos` and `/privacidad` (public, indexable) and Perfil › Legal y privacidad.
    - Add `terms` and `privacy` versions to `consents`, by date like `RULES_VERSION`.
    - Text to follow.
11. **Remove «modo solo»:** `SoloScreen` and `/solo`. Keep the `solo_session_*` notices; those are the
    one-to-one meeting warning, not «modo solo».
