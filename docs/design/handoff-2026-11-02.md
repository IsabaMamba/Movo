## Decisions taken today

- **Women-only sessions and groups.** Gender is asked once at sign-up: Mujer, Hombre or Prefiero no
  decir. It is visible only to its owner and used for one thing: deciding who sees women-only
  content.
  - **Mujer** sees women-only sessions and groups and joins directly.
  - **Prefiero no decir** sees them and confirms «¿Eres mujer?» once per session or group.
  - **Hombre** never sees them. A shared link shows only «Es solo para mujeres», with no place or time.
  - Women-only includes trans women.
  - Creating, running or organizing anything women-only requires verified identity.
  - «Para quién» appears only when a verified woman creates a standalone session or series; inside a
    group, the session inherits the group's audience.
  - A report confirmed by the team means a suspension with no end date.
  - There is no «men only».
- **Lilac `#C7A6FF` is a hint, never a surface.** Only: the «Solo mujeres» chip, a 3 px left rule on
  the card, a 2 px top line on the detail, the filter chip and the sheet's top border. Never
  buttons, backgrounds, long text or the heat map. The join button stays sun.
- **Identity verification** is the human review decided on 16 Oct, now designed. It does not confirm
  gender — Costa Rican ID cards have not shown sex since 2018 — only that the person exists, matches
  the profile and is 18+.
- **Pasadas keeps 6 months** of detail, then only a monthly count. The copy says the history exists,
  is only for you and is never public.
- **Rules are named by their date** («las normas del 2 de noviembre»), as `RULES_VERSION` does.
- **Recommendations don't exist yet**, so no screen promises them.
- **The street map** is MapLibre plus our own Protomaps extract on Supabase. Steps in
  [`mapa.md`](mapa.md).

## Backend work, in build order

Every new table and function: `revoke all … from public, anon`, RPC-only writes.

1. **Gender, private.** `profile_private.gender` (`mujer` | `hombre` | `no_dice`), readable and
   writable only by its owner, like `home_district` in 0034. Never in public profile queries, rosters
   or the console. Log changes (who, when, from → to). Sign-up step and Editar perfil row as in
   Mujeres v1.
2. **Audience.** `communities.audience` and `activities.audience` (`mixto` | `solo_mujeres`). Discover,
   Grupos and invite-link queries filter on the viewer's gender server-side. `join_community`,
   `join_activity` and `join_series` reject `hombre`, and require `confirmed_woman = true` only for
   `no_dice`, stored on that membership or participation row. Report reason `not_eligible`; a
   confirmed one suspends with no end date.
3. **Identity verification.** `identity_requests` (status, reason, reviewer, timestamps — never the ID
   number). Photos in a private bucket, short-lived signed URLs for `is_staff()` only, every view
   logged. A job deletes the files at decision or after 30 days. New consent purpose
   `identity_verification` in `consents` (0030). `verified_at` / `verified_by` on the profile.
4. **Notices.** The new types in the Avisos v2 catalog, each with its `toInboxItem` case and test, plus
   a per-person preferences table for the ones that can be turned off. Push text stays fixed.
5. **Pasadas, 6 months.** A nightly job like `purge_attendance_history` (0021) that summarizes
   participations older than 6 months into a monthly count.
6. **Street map.** [`mapa.md`](mapa.md).
