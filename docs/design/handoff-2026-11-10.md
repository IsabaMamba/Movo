## Your checklist, answered

- [x] **The «Invítalas a toda la serie» card** — Series v2 · 1–4. It replaces the provisional block in
      «Quiénes van»:
  - on top, one sentence on what happens and «Invitar a las N» (only with 2 or more pending);
  - then «Fecha por fecha · N», each row with its own «Invitar» (44 px);
  - an invited row shows «Invitada» and the day, in green, with no button;
  - people on the whole series collapse into one row («4 van a toda la serie») that opens the list;
  - «Invitar a las N» opens a confirmation with the faces and the exact notice text (3);
  - when everybody is invited, a sentence and no button (4).
- [x] **Copy of the four notices** — table below. Small changes; the destination of «salió de la serie»
      changes from nothing to «Quiénes van».
- [x] **Length of a series** — 8 dates by default, as every board since September assumes; the organizer
      can pick 4 to 12. _Alejandro confirms._
- [x] **Renewing a series** — Series v2 · 9–10. One month before the last date the organizer gets «¿Agregas
      8 fechas más?» with the dates as chips; tapping one removes it (a holiday) and the last moves one week.
      Each person on the whole series is asked «¿Sigues en la serie?» with two answers of equal weight; no
      answer means the series ends for them on the last date. It never renews by itself.
- [x] **The zone sheet** — it works. Three tweaks in Precios v1 · 5–7: sport names in their family text
      colour; «N sesiones · M personas» on each bar, with «menos de 5» per sport too; and «Organizar una
      aquí» in a zone with few sessions.
- [x] **Prices** — Precios v1 · 1–4 and the table on the board. One change to the code: the thousands
      separator is a **point** (₡1.200), not a comma; in Costa Rica a comma reads as a decimal.
- [x] **States without design** — Series v2 · 11–16: a series with no dates left (for someone who went,
      and for someone arriving by an old link); loading as skeletons shaped like the content; a full-screen
      error only when there is nothing to show; stale data with a banner when offline; and a save error next
      to what failed, saying what was and was not saved.

## Changes to what is built

1. **Reasons for leaving:** «Trabajo», «Un viaje», «Algo personal». «Salud» goes: it is sensitive and the
   organizer does not need it. Stored values: `trabajo`, `viaje`, `personal`.
2. **Thousands separator:** `priceLabel()` uses a point with a fixed format, not the device locale.
3. **`series_left`** opens «Quiénes van» for the organizer.
4. **The invitation text** says how many dates are left (needs `remaining` in the payload).

## Backend for renewing (when you get to it)

- `series_renewal_offer` to the organizer one month before the last date, once per series.
- `renew_series(series, dates_to_skip)`: adds 8 dates, minus the skipped ones (stored as exceptions), the
  last moving a week per skip. Never automatic.
- `series_renewal` to each person on the whole series; `answer_series_renewal(series, yes)`. No answer
  means they are not on the new dates.
