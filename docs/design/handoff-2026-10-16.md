## Decisions taken today

- **Navigation.** Perfil replaces «Mi cuenta» as the fifth tab. Organizar is a row inside Perfil.
- **Profile image.** A normal photo the person chooses (reviewed before anyone sees it) **or** the
  sport avatar. One at a time.
- **Check-in is phone-first, done by the participant.** They tap «Llegué» and type the session's
  4-digit code; the organizer sees the list fill in, marks anyone without a phone, reviews and
  closes. The summary arrives as a notice. The code exists because a plain «Llegué» can be tapped
  from home, and attendance is the number the product will one day show sponsors.
- **Who organizes.** Anyone creates a free session. Series and paid sessions require verified
  identity.
- **Empty Mis sesiones.** No more «modo solo» there: «Da el primer paso» — join a session nobody has
  joined yet and invite friends with a link that opens Movo on the web.
- **Details and links.** A free-text box (uses the existing `activities.description`) and up to two
  links to third-party apps for routes. **Temporary**: Movo will later create, save and use routes
  with its own GPS.

## Backend work, in build order

1. **Links as their own field, for every category** — replaces the Running-only `route_url`
   attribute. `https` only; no link shorteners or IP hosts; max 2 per session; store the domain
   separately and always show it; leave a notice for everyone on the roster when a link changes;
   «Reportar este enlace» goes to the existing report queue; URLs typed inside the description are
   never made tappable.
2. **`self_check_in(activity, code)`** — accepted from 15 min before to 45 min after the start;
   5 attempts per person; a per-session 4-digit code the organizer can regenerate; `checked_in_by`
   distinguishes self from organizer; the organizer can undo either before closing.
   `close_activity()` and `remind_unclosed_sessions()` stay as they are. `revoke all … from public,
anon`.
3. **Series sign-up:** `join_series()` (a full date puts the person on that date's waitlist only, and
   says so before confirming) and `leave_series()`. Two `activity_series` flags: allow whole-series
   sign-up, allow single dates. A series-invitation table unique on (series, person), so «Invitarlas
   a la serie» reaches each person once.
4. **Pricing columns** — unit (per person / total split), split total and divisor, venue entry fee
   (none / amount / unknown). Show it as «₡1,200 por persona · cancha ₡12,000 entre 10». In a series
   the price is per date, and says so.
5. **`zone_summary(zone, category, days)`** for the zone sheet in Descubrir — aggregates only; show
   «menos de 5» below five people.
6. **Profile photo** — automated moderation before it is shown, private bucket, an Edge Function
   strips EXIF and is the only writer of `avatar_url` (clients still can't write it, as in 0024).
7. **Language in code** — «apunt…» appears 41 times in 13 files and «Mejenga» in `GruposScreen`
   and tests. Suggest extending `check:voice` to fail on «apunt», «mejenga» and «¿te vienes», and to
   scan `supabase/` as well as `src/`.

## Still open

- **Check-in without signal.** Parks often have none. Queue the tap with its timestamp and send it
  later, or use a time-based code the phone can verify offline. Decide before item 2 is built.
- **How identity is verified.** A verification provider is a new data recipient; ID numbers are never
  stored.
- **Series length.** 8 dates, unconfirmed since 5 September. Renewal: the organizer offers 8 more,
  and each person on the whole series confirms — never automatic.
- **`home_district`** is readable through the API by anyone sharing a session (0025). The public
  profile promises not to show the zone; that promise needs the column closed, not just hidden.
- **New data recipients** to add to the privacy and SDK documents before building: image moderation,
  the AI avatar provider, identity verification.
- **Future GPS routes.** A recorded route is precise location, and it usually starts and ends at
  someone's home. Hide the first and last stretch before showing one.
