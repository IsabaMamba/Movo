# Audit — 7 October 2026

Thirteen reviews run in one day against `main` at `852066e`: privacy, terms, refunds,
cookies, consent UX, consent surfaces, data minimization, SDKs and vendors, dark patterns,
pricing transparency, frontend security, deployment hardening, SEO and indexing.

**This file is the engineering half.** Every item below is a code or configuration change with
a named file. The legal analysis — regulatory exposure, drafting decisions, outstanding
questions for counsel — is deliberately not in this repository, which is public.

Each item says which review found it. Nothing here is a draft of a policy; it is the work the
product needs so that a policy, when written, is true.

---

## What the reviews found overall

Most of what was audited came back clean, and several findings are worth stating as facts to
preserve rather than problems to fix:

- **No cookies, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifiers, no pixels, no fingerprinting, no
  third-party scripts.** Nineteen runtime dependencies, of which exactly one — Supabase —
  transfers user data by design.
- **Fonts are bundled and served from our own origin.** The map is drawn from
  `zone_outlines()` as SVG, with no third-party tile service. Google Maps and Waze are
  outbound links only.
- **No dark patterns.** No confirm shaming, no preselected options, no fake urgency, no
  manufactured scarcity, no unequal buttons.
- **Authorization is in the database where it belongs** — deny-by-default RLS, column grants,
  RPC-only participation, `revoke all` on every function, 16 SQL suites.
- **No mixed content.** Every outbound URL is `https://`.

The gaps are concentrated in three places: **things that do not exist yet** (account deletion),
**things that exist and are not wired** (dead schema, an unused helper), and **configuration
that has outlived its purpose**.

## P0 — before the first public session

### 1 · Account deletion and data export

**Five reviews raised this independently.** There is no deletion path anywhere in the
codebase; `Mi cuenta` has a name field, sign-out and links.

It is a legal obligation that already applies to our Costa Rican users, and in UX terms it is
a roach motel: a person can create an account and cannot leave.

Two decisions have to be made first, and they are product decisions, not legal ones:

- **A deleted user's messages in group chats** — removed, or retained as «usuario eliminado»?
- **`reports` naming a deleted user** — do they survive? Safety argues yes; privacy argues no.
  The likely answer is "retained, de-identified", but it must be decided and written down.

Export can be a JSON of the user's own rows. It does not need a UI beyond a button.

### 2 · The `staff` rota

`staff` holds two accounts and both are Kristopher's; one is the `Lee sin` test account.
`/normas` promises every user that a person reads each report within 24 hours.

Three statements in the Supabase panel: add Alejandro, remove `Lee sin`, update the name in
`docs/security.md`. Open since 17 September.

A test account should not be able to read every safety report.

### 3 · `noindex` on every gated route

_(SEO review.)_ `app.json` sets `web.output: "static"`, so all 17 routes pre-render to their
own HTML. Fourteen require an account and none carries a robots directive. There is no
`robots.txt`.

A crawler gets the shell and not the data — rendering happens after auth — so this is index
pollution and URL disclosure rather than content leakage. It is still wrong, and it is cheap.

- `noindex, nofollow` on all 14 gated routes
- `public/robots.txt` with the gated paths disallowed
- **`/sesion/[id]` must never put the session name, venue or time in `<title>`.** A tab title
  is visible over a shoulder, lands in history, and is what a screenshot captures
- A test asserting every gated route carries `noindex` — the pattern `check-voice` and
  `check-migrations` already established

### 4 · Security headers at the first deployment

_(Frontend security, deployment hardening.)_ Session tokens live in `localStorage` on web,
which is normal for a static Expo build — but the Content-Security-Policy is the control that
makes it acceptable, and there is no host yet to carry one.

The full header set is specified in the deployment review. The load-bearing line is
**`script-src 'self'` with no `unsafe-inline`.** The day someone wants to drop in an analytics
snippet, the pressure will be to relax it; that trade is the whole risk.

## P1 — remove what should not be there

### 5 · Dead schema

_(Data minimization.)_

- **`device_tokens`** — created in `0001`, granted `select, insert, update, delete` to
  `authenticated` in `0003`, **referenced by zero lines of application code.** ADR 0007
  replaced Expo push with Web Push and `push_subscriptions` took over. A client-writable table
  of device identifiers for a feature that no longer exists. **Drop it.**
- **`profile_private.phone`** and **`emergency_contact`** — no UI writes them, nothing reads
  them. The emergency contact is a third party's personal data for a feature nobody built.
  **Either build the feature with a stated purpose and retention, or drop the columns.**
  "Leave them for later" is the option that causes the incident.
- **`profile_private.birthdate`** — equally unused, but **keep it.** It is how the 18+ rule
  gets enforced (item 9). Give it a job.

### 6 · Stale Mapbox configuration

_(Frontend security.)_ `.env.example` carries `EXPO_PUBLIC_MAPBOX_TOKEN` with a comment about
map tiles. **Mapbox is not used anywhere** — not in `package.json`, not in `src/`.

The risk is not the empty variable. `.env.example` is the file a new contributor fills in, and
this line tells them to go and provision a token that would ship in the client bundle and add
a third-party recipient to a product that currently has none. **Delete the three lines.**

### 7 · `web-push@3.6.7` is unmonitored

_(SDK review.)_ Pinned as an `npm:` specifier inside `supabase/functions/send-push`.
**Dependabot cannot see it and `npm audit` does not cover it** — it is not in `package.json`.
It is a cryptography library, and the only dependency in the audit that runs in production.

Put it on a review date.

### 8 · Message retention

_(Data minimization.)_ `messages` are soft-deleted via `deleted_at` and **never purged.**
Attendance history — the carefully fenced, far less sensitive dataset — expires at 90 days on
pg_cron.

The asymmetry is hard to defend. `purge_attendance_history()` already proves the mechanism;
this is one more job.

## P2 — correctness and transparency

### 9 · Enforce the 18+ minimum

_(Consent audit.)_ `/normas` and sign-up both say 18. Nothing checks it. Ask for a birthdate at
sign-up and refuse under-18 at the form rather than suspending afterwards. Uses the column
item 5 says to keep.

### 10 · Record consent

_(Consent audit, consent UX.)_ `attendance_history_consent` in `0021` does this correctly —
timestamp plus `ATTENDANCE_NOTICE_VERSION`. Push and location record nothing, and nothing
stores which `RULES_VERSION` a user accepted.

One `consents` table modelled on `0021`, covering push, location and rules acceptance. One
migration, and it is what turns "they agreed" into "they agreed to this text, on this date".

### 11 · A «Tus permisos» screen

_(Consent UX.)_ Three consents live on three different screens with no single place to review
them. One screen in Mi cuenta listing all of them, each with its current state and one control
to change it.

**Do not build a cookie banner.** There is nothing non-essential to consent to, and a banner
asking for consent to nothing trains people to dismiss the next one.

### 12 · Pricing: venue fees and the price unit

_(Pricing transparency.)_ Two live gaps on a surface users already see:

- **`locations` has `currency` but no price column**, so a venue's own entry fee cannot be
  recorded or shown. A session reads ₡2.000 and the user pays the gate as well. Add
  `entry_price_minor` and display the combined total as approximate. **A venue with no
  recorded fee shows «no sabemos», never «Gratis»** — the same rule `priceLabel` already
  follows for sessions.
- **«Precio» does not say per person or total.** A cancha rental is naturally a total; a
  coached class is naturally per-person. `pricePerPerson()` exists in `src/theme/index.ts`,
  is unit-tested with four cases, and **is called by no screen.** Add the choice at entry and
  wire up the helper.

Also worth one line in the create form, under the price field: «Lo que pongas aquí es lo único
que puedes cobrar. Cobrar de más en el lugar es motivo de suspensión.» That turns `/normas`
into a sentence at the moment the price is set.

### 13 · Email tracking, when SMTP is configured

_(Cookies review.)_ SendGrid, Mailgun and Resend enable open and click tracking **by default**.
Confirmation email is mandatory to register, so a person cannot refuse a tracking pixel in it
and still use Movo.

**Turn tracking off for transactional mail.** Marketing mail is a separate stream with its own
opt-in.

### 14 · Verify leaving a group

_(Dark patterns.)_ `docs/status.md` lists under _Not verified_: "Leaving a group — Aguita is
still a member." Sessions exit in one tap. Groups may not. One test; build the exit if it is
missing.

## Prevention

Three of today's findings share one shape: **something was written, tested, and never
connected.** `device_tokens`, `pricePerPerson()`, and the `phone` / `emergency_contact`
columns.

Two questions on the review checklist would have caught all three:

- **For a new column or table:** what fills this, what reads it, when is it deleted?
- **For a new dependency:** what does it send, where does it go, what breaks if we remove it?

`0014`'s own review caught two real defects by asking the equivalent questions about grants.
This is the same discipline applied to data and to vendors.
